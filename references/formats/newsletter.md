# Newsletter Format Reference

Structural rules for newsletter content.

---

## Length
800–2,000 words per edition depending on type.

## Structure
- Editorial intro (2-3 paragraphs, sets the theme)
- Featured content sections (2-4, each with context and link)
- Market intelligence section (signals, trends)
- Closing note (forward-looking, personal)

## Tone
More conversational than blog posts. The newsletter is a curated conversation with your audience, not a publication.

## CTA
Each section can have its own micro-CTA. The edition has one primary CTA.
