### Added
- 24 AI agent skill definitions covering the full GTM content pipeline
- 11 configurable GTM frameworks (ICP, content pillars, pipeline, signals, etc.)
- Voice profiles and format specifications for multiple content channels
- PostgreSQL database schema (20+ tables across framework, pipeline, intelligence, and learning layers)
- Templates for creating custom agents, frameworks, and voice profiles
- Full documentation (architecture, setup, customisation, deployment, agent reference)
