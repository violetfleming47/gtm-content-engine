# ICP Pain Framework — VoC Scanner Reference

Condensed from your ICP Framework (`frameworks/icp-framework.md`). Contains the pain points, titles, and industry context the scanner needs to identify who is speaking and what they're frustrated about.

Populate this by answering the questions below for each of your ICP personas.

---

## How to populate

For each persona type (you should have 3–10), fill in:

1. **Who they are** — one sentence describing this persona's role
2. **Titles to recognise** — split into Senior (Economic Buyer) and Operational (Champion)
3. **Pain points** — split into Strategic/budget pain and Operational/day-to-day pain
4. **Industry nuances** — how this persona differs across your target sectors
