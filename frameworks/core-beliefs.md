## CB-01: Evaluation Speed Is Existential

**belief_slug:** `evaluation-speed-existential`

**belief_statement:** Organisations that take 3–18 months and hundreds of thousands of dollars to evaluate a single vendor are not cautious — they are competitively exposed. In the AI era, the institution that validates in weeks will outrun the institution that validates in quarters.

**counter_positions:** "Thorough evaluation protects the institution." "Speed introduces risk." "Our process exists for good reason." The traditional view frames slowness as diligence. [YOUR COMPANY] frames it as a failure mode — the risk of not moving is greater than the risk of moving carefully but fast.

## CB-03: Synthetic Data Unlocks Safe Testing

**belief_slug:** `synthetic-data-unlocks`

**belief_statement:** High-fidelity synthetic data — structured, semi-structured, and unstructured — lets institutions get meaningful, production-representative test results without ever touching real PII. ## CB-22: AI Governance and Data Governance Are Separate Problems

**belief_slug:** `decouple-ai-and-data-governance`

**belief_statement:** [Target organisations] treat "can I use this AI tool?" and "can I access this data?" as the same approval process. They're not. [YOUR COMPANY]'s approach — synthetic data in an air-gapped environment — deliberately decouples them. The AI evaluation gate (does this tool work?) resolves in the [evaluation environment] with synthetic data. The data access gate (can we connect to real data?) resolves separately, after technology validation. Conflating these two gates is what turns a two-week evaluation into a six-month slog. This is the mechanism behind CB-01, CB-03, and CB-12.

**counter_positions:** "You can't evaluate an AI tool without seeing how it handles our real data." "Our compliance process requires both assessments together." [YOUR COMPANY]'s position: whether a tool can handle specific data structures, formats, and edge cases is determinable using synthetic data. The real-data test is a production readiness gate, not an evaluation gate.

## CB-23: Metadata Is More Valuable Than Data for Early-Stage Experimentation

**belief_slug:** `metadata-over-data`

**belief_statement:** Actual [industry data] is not needed to determine whether a vendor can handle an institution's data structure, edge cases, and document formats. Headers, data dictionaries, and schema definitions are enough to generate high-fidelity synthetic data that gives a valid signal. "Tell me about your data" not "give me your data." Evidence: [Example Company C]'s Dexter process. Operationalises CB-03 and CB-22. Metadata is sufficient for experimentation; actual rows and columns are a production concern.

**counter_positions:** "You can't test meaningfully without real data." "Our data has unique characteristics that synthetic data can't replicate." [YOUR COMPANY]'s position: the unique characteristics are captured in the metadata (schema, distributions, edge cases). The actual values are not what makes testing meaningful — the structure and variety are.
