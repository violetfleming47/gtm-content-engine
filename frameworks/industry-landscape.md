## Forces Driving Change

These are the structural pressures that make [YOUR COMPANY]'s market grow. They don't change week to week but they explain *why* signals matter.

**[Persona E]y pressure on AI governance.** [Persona E]s worldwide are demanding that institutions demonstrate responsible AI adoption. This requires structured evaluation, documentation, and auditability — all of which [evaluation environment] infrastructure enables.

**The build-vs-buy tension.** Engineering teams believe they can build evaluation infrastructure internally. Innovation teams know they can't wait. This internal friction drives platform adoption when the cost of the status quo becomes visible.

**Talent scarcity.** There aren't enough engineers, data scientists, and AI specialists to support the volume of evaluation work institutions need. Platform infrastructure that multiplies what small teams can do addresses this directly.

**Competitive pressure.** Institutions that evaluate and adopt technology faster gain competitive advantage. The fear of falling behind peers is a powerful motivator — particularly when a competitor's AI adoption becomes visible.

**Cloud maturation.** As institutions complete cloud migration, the next question is: what do we do with this infrastructure? AI experimentation and vendor evaluation are top use cases. Hyperscaler partnerships create ecosystem dynamics.

**Post-pandemic digital acceleration.** The urgency around digital transformation hasn't receded. Boards that approved digital strategies during COVID are now asking for measurable outcomes. Innovation teams need to demonstrate throughput and ROI.
