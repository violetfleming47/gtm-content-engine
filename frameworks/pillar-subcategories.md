# F5b: Content Pillar Sub-Categories

Framework for `pillar_subcategories` table. Defines how content pillars (see F3) are expressed in practice. A sub-category answers: "What kind of content is this?" Consumed by Ghost Writer (what to write), Content Distributor (categorisation), Scorer (pillar alignment), Content Suggester (gap detection).

Sub-categories are distinct from content types (see F5a). A sub-category is what the content is. A content type is how it is packaged. Example: a case study (sub-category) can be packaged as a carousel, a long-form text post, or a blog article (content types).

Each sub-category maps to one or more content pillars and lists the content types it is typically packaged in.

---

## Case Study

What it is: A structured narrative documenting a specific engagement, project, or outcome. Follows a pattern: context/challenge, approach, outcome, lessons learned. Named or anonymised. Evidence-heavy — metrics, timelines, specific details.

Typical pillars: KP-04 Notes from the Field, KP-03 Social Proof (when named), NP-04 Enterprise AI Tech (when vendor evaluation is the focus), NP-05 [YOUR PRODUCT] (when [evaluation environment] experimentation is the focus).

Typical content types: Carousel (challenge → approach → outcome → lesson maps directly to a swipeable slide sequence), long-form text post, blog post / article, one-pager (condensed version), email newsletter.

---

## Hot Take / Market Commentary

What it is: A short, opinionated reaction to a specific event, announcement, trend, or piece of news. Time-sensitive — published within hours of the trigger event. Value comes from speed and perspective, not depth.

Typical pillars: KP-02 Market Event Triggered, NP-03 Ecosystem Events / News.

Typical content types: Short-form text post, long-form text post (if the event warrants deeper analysis), thread (on X/Twitter).

---

## Behind-the-Scenes

What it is: Content showing internal process, team activity, workspace, decision-making, or day-to-day reality. Informal, unpolished. The value is authenticity and access, not production quality.

Typical pillars: NP-01 Persona / Personal, KP-06 Bigger Cause + Human.

Typical content types: Short-form text post (the story), long-form text post (the reflection), infographic (team milestone visualised).

---

## Social Proof / Testimonial

What it is: Content featuring a client quote, partner endorsement, award, milestone, or third-party validation. The proof comes from someone other than the author — the author is presenting evidence, not making claims. Named institutions and specific outcomes carry the most weight.

Typical pillars: KP-03 Social Proof.

Typical content types: Short-form text post, carousel (multiple proof points compiled), infographic (milestone visualised), blog post / article (award or partnership announcement with context).

---

## Vendor Analysis / Shout-Out

What it is: Content evaluating, highlighting, or commenting on a specific vendor, tool, or technology. Framed as evaluation and education, not promotion. Explains what the technology does, who it is and isn't for, and how it performed under real conditions.

Typical pillars: NP-04 Enterprise AI Tech, NP-06 Marketplace.

Typical content types: Short-form text post, long-form text post, carousel (vendor comparison or evaluation framework), infographic (landscape map or comparison matrix).

---

## Framework / How-To

What it is: Content that teaches a concept, process, or decision-making framework. Structured, educational, actionable. The reader should be able to apply the framework after consuming the content.

Typical pillars: KP-01 Enterprise AI Adoption, NP-02 Agentic AI (when teaching how agentic systems work in practice), NP-05 [YOUR PRODUCT] (when teaching experimentation methodology).

Typical content types: Carousel (the natural format — each step or principle is a slide), long-form text post, blog post / article, infographic (the framework visualised), one-pager / cheat sheet (the condensed reference), white paper (the comprehensive version).

---

## Customer Story / Field Learning

What it is: A narrative grounded in a real enterprise engagement. Less structured than a formal case study — may focus on a single moment, blocker, decision, or unexpected outcome rather than the full challenge-to-outcome arc. The emphasis is on the learning, not the showcase.

Typical pillars: KP-04 Notes from the Field.

Typical content types: Long-form text post (narrative), short-form text post (one specific insight), carousel (multiple learnings compiled).

---

## Industry / Ecosystem Commentary

What it is: Content analysing trends, shifts, or developments at the industry or ecosystem level. Not tied to a single event (that's hot take) — this is pattern recognition across multiple signals. "Where the market is going" rather than "what happened today."

Typical pillars: NP-03 Ecosystem Events / News, KP-01 Enterprise AI Adoption (when the trend affects adoption strategy).

Typical content types: Long-form text post, blog post / article, carousel (trend breakdown), infographic (market map or data visualisation).

---

## Contrarian / Myth-Busting

What it is: Content that challenges a widely held assumption, conventional wisdom, or common practice. The value is in the disagreement — supported by evidence, experience, or logic. "Everyone says X. Here's why that's wrong."

Typical pillars: KP-01 Enterprise AI Adoption, NP-02 Agentic AI, NP-04 Enterprise AI Tech. Cross-cuts multiple pillars depending on the belief being challenged — often directly expresses a core belief from F2.

Typical content types: Short-form text post (punchy), long-form text post (argued), carousel (myth vs. reality structure).

---

## Platform / Product Explainer

What it is: Content that explains what [YOUR COMPANY] does, how it works, or what happens inside the platform. Framed as education, not sales. Shows the mechanism — [evaluation environment], marketplace, synthetic data, evaluation process — without reading as a product data sheet.

Typical pillars: KP-05 [YOUR COMPANY], NP-05 [YOUR PRODUCT], NP-06 Marketplace.

Typical content types: Carousel (walkthrough), long-form text post, blog post / article, infographic (platform architecture or process visualised), one-pager.

---

## Bigger Picture / Mission

What it is: Content about outcomes at ecosystem, government, or sector level. Enterprise AI maturity, responsible adoption, sector-level impact, government engagement, long-term implications. "Shaping how AI is adopted, not just used."

Typical pillars: KP-06 Bigger Cause + Human.

Typical content types: Long-form text post, blog post / article, short-form text post (single reflection).

---

## Team / Culture

What it is: Content about the people — new hires, team milestones, social events, public recognition, delivery moments, learning moments. The human side of the company.

Typical pillars: NP-01 Persona / Personal, KP-06 Bigger Cause + Human.

Typical content types: Short-form text post, long-form text post (reflection on a team moment).

---

## Meme / Humour

What it is: A visual or text post designed to be funny, relatable, or satirical. Low production, high relatability. Works when the humour is industry-specific and demonstrates insider knowledge — if the joke only lands for people in enterprise AI, it's a niche authority signal.

Typical pillars: NP-01 Persona / Personal (engagement driver, pattern breaker).

Typical content types: Short-form text post, infographic (image with text overlay).

---

## AMA / Q&A

What it is: A post inviting the audience to ask questions on a specific topic, with the author responding in comments. The value is in the replies, not the original post. Best when scoped to a specific topic.

Typical pillars: Cross-cuts multiple pillars depending on the topic scoped. KP-01 Enterprise AI Adoption, NP-02 Agentic AI, NP-05 [YOUR PRODUCT] are the most natural fits.

Typical content types: Short-form text post (the prompt), with the substance generated in comments. Strong Q&A threads can be repurposed as a blog post, carousel, or series of short-form text posts.

---

## Data / Research Highlight

What it is: Content that surfaces a specific data point, statistic, research finding, or benchmark. Not the full report — a single compelling finding with context and implications. "This number changed how I think about X."

Typical pillars: KP-01 Enterprise AI Adoption, NP-02 Agentic AI, NP-03 Ecosystem Events / News, NP-04 Enterprise AI Tech. Depends on the data source and topic.

Typical content types: Short-form text post (the number + the implication), infographic (the data visualised), carousel (multiple findings from a single source).

---

## Consortium / Partnership Update

What it is: Content about consortium activity, partner engagement, institutional collaboration, or ecosystem development. Evidence that [YOUR COMPANY] is embedded in the infrastructure, not just selling to it.

Typical pillars: KP-03 Social Proof, KP-06 Bigger Cause + Human, NP-03 Ecosystem Events / News.

Typical content types: Short-form text post, long-form text post, blog post / article.
