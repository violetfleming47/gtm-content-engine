**How to use this document:**
This document captures the eight dimensions missing from the ICP Framework that are needed for automated profiling, scoring, and content matching. Each dimension is split by Economic Buyer and Champion because they use different language, consume different content, and respond to different framing.

**The eight dimensions:**
1. Language Patterns — vocabulary, phrases, and jargon they use when describing their problems.
2. Content Preferences — what formats and types they engage with, share, and save.
3. What Resonates — the framing, evidence, and tone that lands.
4. What Repels — what turns each persona off.
5. Authority Signals — what makes them trust you. Proof points, credentials, and behaviours that establish credibility.
6. Typical Messaging — how they describe their own challenges to peers and on LinkedIn.
7. Preferred Tone — formal vs conversational, data-heavy vs narrative, provocative vs measured.
8. [YOUR COMPANY] Relevance Framing — how [YOUR COMPANY]'s value connects to this persona's specific pain. Not the use case list — the narrative.
