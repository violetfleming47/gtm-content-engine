**[YOUR COMPANY] ICP Framework**
Identifying Buyers in the Ecosystem
Five persona types. When you meet someone, place them in one.

**How to use this framework:**
Each category is a type of person you will encounter in a deal. They are defined by what they do and what they care about. Everyone you meet should fit into one of these five. For each:

•  Economic Buyer — who controls the budget. You need them but they rarely drive the deal.
•  Champion — who drives the deal day to day. They feel the pain, they found you, they push internally.
•  Economic Buyer Titles — what the economic buyer is called across different organisations.
•  Champion Titles — what the champion is called across different organisations.
•  Economic Buyer Pain Points — what the economic buyer worries about. Strategic, budgetary, and reputational.
•  Champion Pain Points — what the champion deals with daily. Operational, process-level, and visceral.
•  Use Cases — what they want to do with the platform. Use to scope.
•  Economic Buyer Lifestyle — how the economic buyer spends their time and what resonates with them.
•  Champion Lifestyle — how the champion spends their time and what resonates with them.
•  Industry Nuances — how the persona differs depending on the industry they sit in.
•  Communication & Engagement Profile — how they talk, what content they engage with, what resonates, what repels, how to frame [YOUR COMPANY]’s value. Feeds profiling, scoring, and outreach.
